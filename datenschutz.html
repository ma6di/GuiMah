2. Hosting

If you send us an enquiry via the contact form, we process the data you enter (name, email address, message) to handle your request. The form is submitted via Formspree (Formspree, Inc.), which acts as a processor for delivering the message to us. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual steps) and/or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to enquiries). We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to process your request, unless a longer retention is required by law.

4. Fonts

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited). When the fonts are requested, your browser may establish a connection to Google servers, which can involve processing of your IP address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a consistent presentation). More information: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Language preference

We store your language choice (EN/DE) in your browser’s local storage so the preference persists across visits. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a usable bilingual interface).

6. Cookies & analytics

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) on this website.
